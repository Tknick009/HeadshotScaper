Upload Meet Entry CSV

Upload a CSV from Hy-Tek Meet Manager or any CSV with School and Athlete Name columns. The app will automatically find each school's roster page, match athletes by name (with fuzzy matching for nicknames), download their headshots, remove backgrounds, and give you clean transparent PNGs.

Supported CSV Formats

  • Hy-Tek Meet Manager — direct export from Meet Manager
  • Simple format — columns: School, Athlete Name
  • Header format — columns with headers like School, Name, First, Last

Full Roster Mode

  • Check Full Roster to download every athlete from each school's roster page
  • The CSV identifies which schools to scrape — all roster athletes are downloaded regardless of whether they appear in the CSV
  • Great for getting complete team headshot sets for video boards, broadcasts, and programs
